Our Story

Passion for Craft,
Pride in Every Joint

Founded in 2005, AA Carpentry has grown from a one-man shop into a full-service woodworking and renovation company serving homeowners and contractors across Canada. Every project—no matter the size—receives the same dedication to quality, detail, and client satisfaction.

We source the finest sustainable hardwoods and use both traditional joinery techniques and modern precision tools to deliver results that stand the test of time.

Our Simple Process

    Consultation
